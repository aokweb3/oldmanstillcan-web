Enter your token ID to pull the full-resolution image straight from IPFS — the same decentralized storage the contract points to. No wallet connection needed.

How to find your Token ID

  1. Go to opensea.io and click Connect Wallet (top right) with the wallet that holds your Mosciun.
  2. Click your profile icon → your wallet address to see everything you own.
  3. Click into your Mosciun piece to open its page.
  4. Look at the address bar URL — it ends in a number, e.g. /1635. That number is your Token ID.
    Do NOT use the "MOSCIUN #___" number shown on the page itself — that's a different, shuffled display number and won't work here.
  5. Paste that number below and hit Fetch.

Don't see it in your wallet automatically? Some wallets don't show every NFT by default. Go to the Mosciun collection page on OpenSea directly and manually search/scroll for the piece — match it by your wallet address (click "Owned by" on any item) or by recognizing your art. Once you find your piece, follow step 4 above using its URL.
